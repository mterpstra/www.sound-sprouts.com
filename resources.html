05 · For SLPs & curious parents

Minimal pairs

What are they?

Minimal pairs are sets of words that differ by just one sound in the same position. Speech therapists use them to help reorganize a child's sound system — kids hear how one tiny sound change makes a totally different word.

tea / key thumb / dumb make / cake back / bat

Each set below pulls words from Dede's Pet Shop. /t/ and /d/ are front sounds, which is this book's focus — pairing them with the story context helps kids understand and use them.
